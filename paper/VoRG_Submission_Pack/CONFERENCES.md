## Online / virtual presentation, what the check showed

- **Top-tier IEEE venues (PerCom):** in-person only. Remote and pre-recorded
  presentations are explicitly disallowed.
- **Regional Indian IEEE conferences (ICACCS, ICMACC, ICOSEC, ICSSIT, ICCCI
  tier):** since 2021 these have generally offered **hybrid or fully virtual
  presentation**, and most still do. The catch is that many now require **at
  least one registered author to present (in person or online) for the paper to
  be forwarded to IEEE Xplore**. This is set per edition and is not guaranteed;
  confirm it on the specific edition's "Registration" or "Author Instructions"
  page before you rely on it.
- None of the shortlisted regional conferences had a 2027 virtual-presentation
  policy published at the time of this check, because their 2027 pages are not
  live yet.

## Recommendation

1. **Primary, ICACCS 2027.** Best fit for a UG applied-CS systems paper, Tamil
   Nadu so cost and travel are low, and past editions allowed virtual
   presentation. Open `icaccs.sece.ac.in` in a browser (accept the certificate
   warning) from **mid-October 2026** onward and watch for the 2027 CFP; expect a
   full-paper deadline around November to December 2026.
2. **Backup, ICAAIC 2027 or ICCET 2027.** Both have live CFP pages, broad AI /
   computing scope, and lower bars. Check deadlines in November 2026.
3. **If a 2026 slot is essential, ICMACC 2026 extended track.** Only if the
   extended call is genuinely still open; email the organisers to confirm.
4. **Do not target PerCom 2027.** Wrong topic area, double-blind, highly
   competitive, and in-person only.

## Before submitting to any of them

- Confirm the exact deadline, page limit, template, and **virtual-presentation
  and Xplore-forwarding policy** on the official site.
- Reformat `main_v3.tex` to the venue's class (`IEEEtran` conference is already
  the base; some want A4, some US Letter).
- Budget the registration fee (typically INR 7,000 to 12,000 for a student
  Indian author; virtual registration is often cheaper).
- If double-blind (PerCom, some AI tracks), strip author names and the GitHub URL
  from the submission copy.
- Fill the multi-topic evaluation table before the camera-ready deadline.
